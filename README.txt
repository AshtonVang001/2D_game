===========================================
==================Story====================
===========================================

Long ago, a legion of knights descended
into the cursed depths below, seeking
glory, treasure, and the power hidden
within the ancient dungeon.

None returned.

Their bones now wander the halls as
restless skeletons, their spirits twisted
into vengeful ghosts that haunt the dark.
The deeper you climb, the stronger the
curse becomes.

You are the final knight.

Armed with steel, courage, and whatever
gold you can scavenge, you must battle
through endless chambers, defeat the
fallen, and uncover the fate of those
who came before you.

But the dungeon was never built for escape.

It was built to endure.
